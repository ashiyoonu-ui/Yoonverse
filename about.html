About

Hi, I'm Ashiq — an SEO strategist on a mission to make SEO simple.

Yoonverse is where I share everything I know about search, openly and for free.

I'm Ashiq, an SEO strategist, helping websites get found by the people who need them. Over time I noticed something frustrating: good SEO knowledge is often hidden behind paywalls, confusing jargon or advice that simply doesn't work anymore.

So I started Yoonverse, a free place to learn search engine optimisation the right way. Whether you're a student, a blogger, a freelancer or a small business owner, you'll find clear explanations and practical steps here.

What I believe

Clarity over jargon

If it can't be explained simply, it isn't understood well enough.

People first

Write for humans. Search engines reward what people find useful.

No shortcuts

Sustainable SEO beats tricks that get penalised later.

Free to learn

Knowledge should be shared, not sold back to beginners.

What you'll find here

  • Step-by-step guides on keyword research, content and technical SEO
  • Strategy breakdowns that show why, not just what
  • Checklists and frameworks you can reuse on your own site
  • Answers to real questions from the community
My goal is simple: help you understand SEO well enough that you never need to rely on guesswork.

Have a question or a topic you'd like me to cover? I'd love to hear from you.
