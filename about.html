Hi, I'm Ashiq — an SEO strategist & search analyst making SEO simple.

Yoonverse is where I publish open-access research on semantic search, topical authority, and query analytics.

I'm Ashiq, an SEO strategist and organic search analyst based in India, helping websites get discovered by the audiences searching for them. Over time I noticed something frustrating: rigorous SEO methodology—especially Semantic SEO, Knowledge Graph triples, and Search Console telemetry—is often locked behind expensive courses or buried in gatekept jargon.

So I created Yoonverse (yoonverse.in), an open-access publication to learn search engine optimization from first principles. Whether you're a founder, content strategist, developer, or student, every framework here is structured for immediate execution.
