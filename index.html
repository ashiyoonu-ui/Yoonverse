Search Fundamentals

The four pillars of search engine optimization.

Every successful website is built on four core steps. Master these, and you will understand how any page earns its rank on Google.

Pillar 01

Crawling & Indexing

Before you can rank, search engine crawlers must discover your URLs. Clean internal links, sitemaps, and fast server responses ensure Googlebot indexes your content without errors.

Pillar 02

Search Intent & Keywords

SEO isn't about guessing what to write. It's about finding the exact questions people type into the search box and matching the format they expect to see.

Pillar 03

Helpful Content

Google rewards pages that answer the user's question directly and thoroughly. Clear headings, simple language, and genuine examples keep readers engaged.

Pillar 04

Topic Authority & Trust

Writing one guide isn't enough. By linking related articles together into topical clusters, you signal to Google that your site is an authority on the whole subject.
