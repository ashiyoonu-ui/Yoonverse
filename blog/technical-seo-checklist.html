The Beginner-Friendly Technical SEO Checklist

Technical SEO11 Oct 2026·By Ashiq·8 min read
⚡ Core Semantic Triples Extracted From This Guide
  • [XML Sitemaps & Clean Internal Links] —(maximize)→ [Googlebot Crawl Efficiency]
  • [Canonical Tags] —(consolidate ranking signals across)→ [Duplicate or Parameterized URLs]
  • [Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS)] —(validate)→ [Page Experience & Mobile Usability]

Great content cannot rank if search engines struggle to access or understand your site. Technical SEO makes sure the foundations are solid. Here are ten checks anyone can do.

1. Your site uses HTTPS

Look for the padlock in the address bar. Free services like Cloudflare give you SSL at no cost.

2. Google can index your pages

Search site:yourdomain.com on Google. If important pages are missing, check Search Console's Pages report.

3. You have an XML sitemap

A sitemap lists the pages you want found. Submit it in Search Console under Sitemaps.

4. robots.txt isn't blocking anything important

Visit yourdomain.com/robots.txt. A line like Disallow: / blocks your whole site.

5. Pages load fast

Test with PageSpeed Insights. Compress images, use Visual Semantic SEO best practices, and serve assets over a CDN.

6. The site works well on mobile

Google mainly uses the mobile version of your site for ranking. Text should be readable and buttons easy to tap.

7. Each page has a unique title and meta description

The title tag is one of the clearest relevance signals and is what people see in search results.

8. One clear H1 per page

Use one main heading, then H2s and H3s to structure the content logically.

9. No broken links or redirect chains

Broken links waste crawl effort and frustrate visitors. Fix or redirect them with a single 301.

10. Canonical tags prevent duplicates

If the same content lives at multiple URLs, a canonical tag tells Google which version is the main one.

✅ Do this monthly: open Google Search Console, check the Pages and Core Web Vitals reports, and fix anything new.
Technical SEO doesn't make you rank. It removes the reasons you wouldn't.
